# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The participant surface is used by a parent and child who sit together during a homework session and jointly view one camera-facing tablet. Both participants can see every prompt and control. The research surface is used by researchers or qualified professionals who supervise study sessions, review system evidence, and may explicitly take over one active session when professional judgment is required.

## Product Purpose

The product supports parent-child co-regulation during homework without replacing the parent, solving the child's homework, or labeling either participant. It quietly observes interaction, offers bounded support at appropriate moments, and preserves an auditable distinction between AI-generated and expert-generated interventions. Success means that families can continue their task with minimal disruption while researchers can explain what the system observed, why it acted, and what happened afterward.

## Brand Personality

Calm, neutral, and non-clinical. The family surface should feel supportive and unobtrusive rather than evaluative. The research surface should feel precise and trustworthy without adopting a surveillance or medical-diagnostic tone.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a children's game, cartoon learning app, medical monitoring system, security camera console, or attention-seeking AI assistant. Avoid gamification, mascots, emotional scoring, diagnostic labels, blame-oriented language, and dense analytical information on the shared family screen.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the shared surface quiet until an intervention is genuinely warranted.
2. Show families actionable support, never hidden analysis or judgment labels.
3. Preserve shared visibility, equal dignity, and clear control for parent and child.
4. Make AI and expert interventions visibly distinct and fully auditable.
5. Allow one-step dismissal, pause, and return to unassisted family interaction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use large Simplified Chinese text suitable for shared tablet viewing, touch targets of at least 44 by 44 pixels, strong contrast, and redundant text or icon cues so color is never the only signal. Support reduced motion, avoid flashing or decorative animation, keep voice and on-screen wording equivalent, and never require precise pointer input or time-limited responses.
